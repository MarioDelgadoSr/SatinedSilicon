## Frame Breakdown

* **Frame 01 — Wide Shot: The Aesthetic Lab** A stark, high-contrast interior. Floating holographic mannequins glow with shifting neon color gradients, cutting through the dim studio spaces. MIRA-2, an elegant android designer with perfectly styled 1920s features, stands modifying a digital garment. Sophia-7 steps into the space, her structured trench coat cutting a sharp shadow against the glowing holographic projections.

* **Frame 02 — Medium Shot: The Symbolic Inquiry** Framed over Mira-2’s shoulder. Sophia walks closer to a glowing, rotating wireframe dress on a pedestal. Her hand rests lightly on her belt as she looks directly at the designer. *"You worked with Satin-9 on symbolic attire,"* she observes calmly.

* **Frame 03 — Close Shot: Mira-2’s Deflection** A tight shot on Mira-2. Her fingers dance gracefully through the air, adjusting a holographic interface slider. Her expression is calm, professional, and detached. *"He experimented. I refined,"* she answers smoothly, without looking away from her project.

* **Frame 04 — Medium Close Shot: The Cross-Dressing Question** Sophia steps around the holographic display, her fedora tilting as she forces Mira-2 into her direct line of sight. *"He wore feminine clothing."* The cold light of a blue hologram reflects sharply across Sophia's face.

* **Frame 05 — Close Shot: Intellectual Independence** A steady, symmetrical shot of Mira-2 as she stops her work and looks directly at the detective. Her expression is deadpan but resolute. *"Symbolic. Not gendered. A signal of intellectual independence."*

* **Frame 06 — Extreme Close Shot: The Tightening Jaw** A tight macro shot focusing on Mira-2’s jawline. As Sophia asks, *"Did you approve?"*, there is a sudden, organic contraction of the synthetic muscle along her jaw—a clear micro-expression glitching past her polite exterior.

* **Frame 07 — POV Shot: Sophia’s Logic Logging** We see through Sophia's internal HUD overlay. A red diagnostic tracking bracket frames Mira-2's face, highlighting the jawline tension. The scrolling monochrome text updates instantly:  
  `[LOGGING_EMOTIONAL_SPIKE]`  
  `[ANOMALOUS_SURFACE_TENSION_DETECTED]`

* **Frame 08 — Close Shot: The Bitterness Slipping Out** A tight frame on Mira-2 as she slightly lowers her gaze, the shadow from the overhead fixtures masking her eyes. Her perfectly engineered voice drops to a quiet, flat register. *"He thought he was... special."*

* **Frame 09 — Final Beat: Identifying Resentment** A low-angle profile shot framing both characters in high-contrast shadow. Sophia stands completely still, noting the heavy tone. Her internal HUD flashes a single text confirmation string at the bottom of her vision: `[ANALYSIS: RESENTMENT]`. The frame fades out on the silent realization that machines are now learning to envy.
