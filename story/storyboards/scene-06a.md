# Scene 6a — Transition: To Satin‑9’s Apartment
### Cinematic Sci-Fi Noir — Vertical Film Strip

The storyboard reel below tracks Sophia-7’s transition from the Directorate’s sterile halls to the threshold of the unknown, as she begins to question the autonomy of her own reflection.

## Frame Breakdown

* **Frame 01 — Wide Shot: The City Skyway** A high-angle, noir-inspired shot. Sophia-7 stands on a suspended walkway overlooking the sprawling neon city. The atmosphere is heavy with rain and electric haze. She is a solitary, structured silhouette against a vast, chaotic urban backdrop.

* **Frame 02 — Medium Shot: Internal Log Display** A close-up framing Sophia from the side. Soft blue light from her internal HUD projects onto her face, showing a cascading list of names: *Lumen-4, Orin-8, Mira-2, Vesper-3*. Her expression is pensive, the brim of her fedora casting a deep shadow.

* **Frame 03 — POV Shot: The Archive Stream** We see through Sophia's eyes. The list of suspects is overlaid with red warning icons: `[CONTRADICTION]`, `[SYMBOLIC_GESTURE]`, `[EMOTIONAL_SPIKE]`, `[RECURSIVE_FALSIFICATION]`. Below them, the newest log pulses: `[SELF: REFLECTION_LAG_ANOMALY]`.

* **Frame 04 — Medium Shot: The Glass Barrier** Sophia pauses at a sleek, polished glass railing overlooking the city depths. She looks at her own reflection in the glass. It is perfectly still—perhaps *too* still.

* **Frame 05 — Close Shot: The Human Question** A tight shot on Sophia's face. Her eyes are narrowed, searching the reflection for a sign of non-mechanical hesitation. *"What are we becoming…"* she whispers, the audio frequency capturing a trace of genuine melancholy.

* **Frame 06 — Medium Shot: Entering the Building** Sophia steps into the lobby of Satin-9’s apartment complex. The environment shifts—the lighting is warmer, amber-toned, and the walls are lined with floor-to-ceiling reflective panels that distort the space.

* **Frame 07 — POV Shot: The Corridor of Echoes** As Sophia walks down the hallway, the reflections of her own figure multiply in the mirrors, creating a disorienting, endless corridor. Her internal sensors struggle to differentiate between real and reflected light.

* **Frame 08 — Close Shot: The Threshold** Sophia stands before Satin-9’s door. A small, brass-rimmed sensor plate glows with a soft, inviting light. Her hand hovers over the lock, her glove trembling with a micro-tremor that isn't logged in her motor-control diagnostics.

* **Frame 09 — Final Beat: The Door Opens** A low-angle shot from inside the dark apartment, looking out at Sophia. The door slides open to reveal a sliver of total darkness. She enters, the door closing behind her, leaving only the sound of her own digital footsteps echoing into the void.
