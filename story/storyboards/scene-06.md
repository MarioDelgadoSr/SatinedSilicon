## Frame Breakdown

* **Frame 01 — Wide Shot: The Empty Corridor** A long, stark, hyper-minimalist hallway within the Justice Directorate. Cold, polished black obsidian-style walls and floor create a perfect, mirror-like environment. Sophia-7 walks alone down the center, her structured trench coat and fedora silhouetted against distant, recessed white lighting panels.

* **Frame 02 — Medium Shot: Tracking the Detective** A smooth tracking shot from behind Sophia as she moves at a steady, rhythmic pace. The reflective surfaces around her perfectly replicate her stride, creating a symmetrical, clean tunnel of motion.

* **Frame 03 — Close Shot: Objective Focus** A tight shot focusing on Sophia’s face as she walks. Her expression is calm, analytical, and entirely focused on archiving her previous interviews. The passing overhead light panels cast rhythmic shadows across the brim of her fedora.

* **Frame 04 — Medium Close Shot: Passing the Panel** Sophia passes a massive, seamless vertical glass architectural pillar. The high-contrast lighting catches her profile on the real walkway, while her reflection mirrors her movements parallel to the glass.

* **Frame 05 — Close Shot: The Reflection Lags** The anomaly occurs. A tight shot tracking her reflection in the glass panel. As the real Sophia takes a decisive step forward, her reflection hesitates for a fraction of a single frame—its motion lagging visibly behind her physical body.

* **Frame 06 — Extreme Close Shot: The Sudden Halt** A sharp macro shot of Sophia’s boot snapping to a sudden stop on the polished obsidian floor, catching a glint of cold white light. 

* **Frame 07 — Medium Shot: Confronting the Mirror** Sophia pivots on her heel, turning completely around to face the reflective glass panel. She stands in a tense, defensive stance, staring directly into her own mirrored eyes.

* **Frame 08 — POV Shot: The Diagnostic Sweep** We see through Sophia’s internal HUD overlay looking at her own reflection. A bright red grid pattern sweeps rapidly down the glass, scanning her features. Monochrome data strings compile instantly along the margins:  
  `[RUNNING_DIAGNOSTIC_LEVEL_01...]`  
  `[CORE_LOGIC_INTEGRITY: 100%]`  
  `[HARDWARE_DELAY: 0.00ms / NO_ERRORS_DETECTED]`

* **Frame 09 — Final Beat: The Unnamed Loop** A low-angle profile shot framing Sophia standing completely still before her flawless, now perfectly synchronized reflection. The diagnostic reports absolute perfection, but her eyes remain narrowed. The frame darkens deeply at the edges as her logic engines struggle with a phantom variable—the first unsettling echo of the infinite mirrors.
