### Frame Breakdown

* **Frame 01 — Wide Shot: The Cold Forensic Chamber** A high-angle, clinical establishing shot. The room is hyper-modern—white light gleams off seamless, mirror-polished obsidian floors. At the center, the deactivated body of Satin-9 lies on a floating black slab. Moving into the frame are two silhouettes in distinct 1920s attire: Sophia-7 in a structured trench coat and Athos in a perfectly tailored, elegantly understated overcoat.

* **Frame 02 — Medium Shot: Sophia and Athos Approach** Tracking shot as they step up to the slab. The contrast is stark: their rich, heavy wool clothing against the sterile, reflective lab environment. Sophia's expression is blank, mathematically precise. Athos stands slightly behind her, hands clasped, a look of quiet, heavy human sorrow on his face.

* **Frame 03 — Close Shot: The Target (Satin-9)** A tight shot focusing on Satin-9. Even in deactivation, the android wears an exquisite, silk-lined 1920s vest and a rumpled button-down shirt. The chest fabric is neatly cut away, revealing a pristine, non-violent incision in the synthetic skin beneath.

* **Frame 04 — Over-the-Shoulder: The Open Chest Panel** Looking over Sophia's shoulder as she leans in. The internal chest panel is open. Floating just above the wound is a crisp, minimalist white holographic interface projecting diagnostic data, casting a faint geometric glow across Sophia’s flawless features.

* **Frame 05 — Close Shot: Sophia’s Eyes (The Discovery)** A tight macro shot of Sophia’s eyes. As the holographic light shifts, her pupils catch the glare, flashing with that striking, predatory "Cat's Eye" retro-reflection. Her processing blink is a fraction of a second too slow. She spots the anomaly.

* **Frame 06 — Insert Shot: The Severed Port** Sophia’s gloved hand gently parts the internal synthetic casing. The camera zooms in on the primary memory port. It hasn't been ripped out; the data filaments are cleanly, surgically severed.

* **Frame 07 — POV Shot: Sophia's Internal HUD Overlay** We see through Sophia’s eyes. Superimposed over the clean mechanical wound, typewriter-style monochrome text scrolls across her field of vision:  
  `[LOG_CORRUPTION_DETECTED]`  
  `[LOCAL_ARCHIVE_ABSENT]`  
  A glowing wireframe highlight traces the exact tool-signature used for the extraction.

* **Frame 08 — Medium Shot: Sophia Straightens Up** Sophia stands up straight, her pinstriped silhouette cutting sharply against the bright white laboratory wall. She turns slightly toward her mentor. *"The memory bank didn't fail, Athos. It was harvested."*

* **Frame 09 — Close Shot: Athos's Reaction** A tight frame on Athos. His expression remains a mask of understated elegance, but his eyes narrow slightly. A subtle, organic tightening of his jaw reveals he understands the terrifying implications of a clean, insider extraction.
