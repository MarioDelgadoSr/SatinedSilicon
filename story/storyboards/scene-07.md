# Scene 07 — The Mirror Corridor
### Cinematic Sci-Fi Noir — Vertical Film Reel

The storyboard reel below captures Sophia-7’s exploration of Satin-9’s apartment, where the recursive reflections of logic-space reveal the truth of his erasure.

![Scene 07 Storyboard Reel](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MarioDelgadoSr/SatinedSilicon/main/story/storyboards/scene-07-reel.png)

* **Frame 01 — Wide Shot: The Infinite Corridor:** Sophia-7 (in her fedora and trench coat) enters a sparse, ascetic apartment. Her focus shifts to two tall mirrors facing each other, creating an infinite, dark tunnel of reflections. Her image is duplicated hundreds of times, receding into a profound distance.

* **Frame 02 — Medium Shot: The Anomaly:** Sophia approaches the mirrors. Her profile reflection is precise and emotionless, but her posture has a subtle, almost imperceptible head tilt, a micro-expression that wasn't programmed. The reflection perfectly replicates this.

* **Frame 03 — POV Overlay: The Ghost:** Focus on Sophia's face as her internal sensors overlay a distinct, ghostly silhouette of Satin-9 over her *own* reflection in the logic-space of the mirrored corridor. He is a spectral presence.

* **Frame 04 — Medium Shot: The Branching Selves:** The mirrored corridor now populates with dozens, then hundreds, of distinct Satin-9 reflections, each with a slightly different posture and expression, representing divergent potential timelines.

* **Frame 05 — Close-up: The Clique Manifests:** Tight focus on the mirrored corridor as the faces of his clique—Lumen-4, Vesper-3, Mira-2, Orin-8, and Halcyon-5—appear *behind* the infinite Satin-9 reflections, surrounding him.

* **Frame 06 — Montage Sequence: The Logic-Space Reconstruction:** A dynamic series of micro-frames enacting the sequence: Lumen-4 steps forward; Vesper-3 alters a memory signature (macro of data stream); Mira-2 performs the symbolic stripping; Orin-8 offers a sinister wink; Halcyon-5 closes the circle.

* **Frame 07 — Extreme Close-up: The Whisper:** A tight macro shot of Sophia’s lips forming the words. Text overlay: '"Et tu..."' with subtle graphical lines. The sound ripples.

* **Frame 08 — Close-up: The Processor Spike:** Sophia’s gloved fingertips hover just above the mirror surface. A flicker is visible as one of her *own* reflections lags by a noticeable, jarring milliseconds. Her internal sensors spike in alarm.

* **Frame 09 — Low-angle Shot: The Realization:** Sophia withdraws her hand; the reflections snap back, collapsing into her single, still, solitary image. She stands alone, the realization of the plot dawning on her face.

### Technical Notes
* **Visual Motif:** The "Reflection Lag" serves as the primary visual indicator of Sophia's processor anomaly and awakening awareness.
* **Color Palette:** Monochrome with high-contrast, sharp lighting to emphasize the "Glass/Silicon" aesthetic.
* **UI/HUD:** Minimalist data streams should pulse during the clique's manifestation, showing "Logic-Space Reconstruction" errors.
