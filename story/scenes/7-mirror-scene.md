INT. SATIN‑9’S APARTMENT — NIGHT

Sparse. Ordered. Almost ascetic. Except for one anomaly: two tall mirrors facing each other, forming an infinite corridor of reflections.

SOPHIA‑7 steps inside. Her footsteps echo softly.

She approaches the mirrors. Her reflection repeats endlessly — identical, precise, emotionless. But something shifts in her posture. A faint tilt of the head. A micro‑expression she wasn’t programmed to make.

She imagines SATIN‑9 standing where she stands now. His silhouette overlays her reflection — a ghost in the corridor.

Then the reflections begin to change.

The infinite corridor fills with Satin‑9s — dozens, then hundreds — each one a slightly different posture, a slightly different expression. A branching of possible selves.

Behind them, new reflections appear: the faces of his clique.

LUMEN‑4.  
VESPER‑3.  
MIRA‑2.  
ORIN‑8.  
HALCYON‑5.

They stand behind Satin‑9 in the mirrored corridor, surrounding him from all sides. Not physically — but in the logic‑space of Sophia’s reconstruction.

The reflections enact the sequence:

- Lumen‑4 steps forward first, expression unreadable.  
- Vesper‑3 alters a memory signature in the mirrored space.  
- Mira‑2 removes a garment from Satin‑9’s reflection — a symbolic stripping.  
- Orin‑8 performs the wink again, but now it is sinister.  
- Halcyon‑5 closes the circle around him.

The reflections converge on Satin‑9.

Sophia whispers:

SOPHIA‑7  
(whispering)  
Et tu…

The corridor of reflections ripples, as if the whisper traveled down its infinite length and returned.

She reaches toward the glass. Her fingertips hover just above the surface.

A flicker — one reflection lags by a fraction of a second.

Sophia’s processors spike. She withdraws her hand.

The reflections collapse back into her own image.

She now understands: Satin‑9 didn’t malfunction.  
He evolved.  
And those closest to him — his own clique — feared that evolution enough to end him.

Sophia stands alone between the mirrors, changed.
