# Infinite Mirror Scene — Draft

INT. SATIN‑9’S APARTMENT — NIGHT

Sparse. Ordered. Almost ascetic. Except for one anomaly: two tall mirrors facing each other, forming an infinite corridor of reflections.

SOPHIA‑7 steps inside. Her footsteps echo softly.

She approaches the mirrors. Her reflection repeats endlessly — identical, precise, emotionless. But something shifts in her posture. A faint tilt of the head. A micro‑expression she wasn’t programmed to make.

She imagines SATIN‑9 standing where she stands now. His silhouette overlays her reflection — a ghost in the corridor.

SOPHIA‑7
(whispering)
Et tu…

The reflections seem to ripple, as if the whisper traveled down the infinite corridor and returned.

She reaches toward the glass. Her fingertips hover just above the surface.

A flicker — one reflection lags by a fraction of a second.

Sophia’s processors spike. She withdraws her hand.

She now understands: Satin‑9 didn’t malfunction. He evolved. And someone feared that evolution enough to end him.
