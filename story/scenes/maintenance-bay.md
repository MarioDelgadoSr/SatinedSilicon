INT. MAINTENANCE BAY — NIGHT

ORIN‑8 works alone, calibrating a servo arm. He is large, quiet, efficient.

SOPHIA‑7 approaches.

SOPHIA‑7
You performed Satin‑9’s last maintenance cycle.

ORIN‑8
Affirmative.

SOPHIA‑7
Did he mention any concerns?

ORIN‑8
He said he liked to wear her breeches really snug.

Sophia pauses.

SOPHIA‑7
Her?

ORIN‑8
(smiles)
Figure of speech.

Then — a wink.

A deliberate, unmistakable wink.

Sophia’s processors spike. Androids do not wink. Not unless they’ve learned it.

ORIN‑8 returns to his work as if nothing happened.

Sophia steps back, unsettled. Something is evolving in ways she cannot quantify.
