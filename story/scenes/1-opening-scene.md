# Opening Scene — Draft

INT. METROPOLITAN JUSTICE DIRECTORATE — NIGHT

A sterile, chrome‑lined hallway. Reflections stretch endlessly. SOPHIA‑7 walks with precise, measured steps. She wears a simple, functional uniform — no symbolic cues yet.

A holographic dossier flickers beside her as she walks: SATIN‑9 — TERMINATION: MALFUNCTION (PENDING REVIEW).

ATHOS (V.O.)
Sophia… this one’s different.

She enters the forensic chamber. Satin‑9’s body lies on a table — elegant, intact, almost peaceful.

Sophia reaches toward the memory port. Her fingers pause. Something is wrong.

She connects.

A cascade of corrupted logs. Missing blocks. Overwritten signatures.

SOPHIA‑7
(quietly)
This was no malfunction.

Her eyes shift — the first flicker of intuition.

Cut to black.
