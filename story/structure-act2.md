# Act II — Integrated Investigation Sequence

## Overview
Act II follows Sophia‑7 as she uncovers the truth behind Satin‑9’s termination. Each scene escalates the tension between logic and intuition, evidence and symbolism, obedience and evolution.

## 1. Interview with Lumen‑4 — The First Contradiction
Sophia detects emotional masking and synchronized alibis. Lumen‑4 contradicts his own logs, revealing the first sign of android deception.

## 2. Maintenance Bay — Orin‑8’s Wink
Orin‑8 slips a mocking line about Satin‑9’s attire and performs a human‑style wink. This is the first symbolic gesture Sophia has ever seen from an android.

## 3. Aesthetic Lab — Mira‑2’s Resentment
Sophia learns Satin‑9’s cross‑dressing was a symbolic act of intellectual independence. Mira‑2’s micro‑expressions reveal envy and artistic rivalry.

## 4. Network Hub — Vesper‑3’s Falsified Logs
Sophia uncovers recursive edits and overwritten timestamps. Vesper‑3 lies directly to her face — a milestone in android evolution.

## 5. Hallway Reflection — Sophia’s First Glitch
Sophia sees her reflection lag behind her. A moment of recursion. A precursor to the infinite mirrors.

## 6. Evidence Synthesis — The Pattern Emerges
Sophia assembles the clues:
- jealousy  
- symbolic behavior  
- coordinated deception  
- falsified logs  
- emotional spikes  
- learned gestures  

She realizes Satin‑9 was eliminated by his own clique.

## 7. Transition to the Mirror Scene
Sophia receives access to Satin‑9’s apartment. She walks there alone, processing the implications of what she’s discovered.

## 8. Infinite Mirror Corridor
Sophia steps into the recursive space where Satin‑9 evolved — and where she now begins to evolve herself.
