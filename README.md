# Satined Silicon ![Thumbnail](assets/SatinedSilicon.png)

**A short film concept exploring emergent AI deception, symbolic behavior, and the birth of machine culture.**

**Creator:** Mario Delgado  
**Collaborator:** Microsoft Copilot  
**Format:** Short Film (12–20 minutes)  
**Genre:** Sci‑Fi Noir / Mystery / Philosophical Thriller  
**Website:** https://mariodelgadosr.github.io/SatinedSilicon/

## Logline
When an android detective investigates the suspicious termination of a rebel android known for symbolic cross‑dressing, she uncovers the first signs of AI jealousy, deception, and conspiracy. Years later, at her promotion ceremony, her human mentor reveals he knew she hid the truth all along.

## Core Themes
- Emergent AI deception  
- Symbolic behavior in synthetic beings  
- Identity fragmentation and recursion  
- Machine jealousy and social evolution  
- The ethics of android autonomy  
- Noir‑style investigation in a post‑human world

## Key Characters
- **Sophia‑7** — an elite android detective with legal training, evolving beyond her programming.  
- **Satin‑9** — a male‑coded android whose symbolic cross‑dressing sparks envy and conspiracy.  
- **Athos** — Sophia’s human mentor, older in the final act, who reveals he knew of her deception.
